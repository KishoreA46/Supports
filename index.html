01

Overview & Philosophy

Why WidgetPDF is designed offline-first

WidgetPDF is built with a simple mission: to provide an exceptional, clean, and distraction-free PDF reading experience with customizable Android home screen widgets.

Unlike many reading apps, WidgetPDF does not require an account, has no subscription paywalls tracking your reading time, and does not upload your documents to any remote servers. Your personal reading habits, bookmarks, and documents remain strictly private to you on your device.

02

Zero Data Collection

No personal data, analytics, or tracking

WidgetPDF adheres to a strict zero-data-collection policy:

  • No Personal Identification: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Document Content or Metadata: The PDFs you read, their contents, filenames, and notes are never transmitted anywhere.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not embed analytics SDKs, behavioral telemetry, advertising trackers, or device identifiers.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: WidgetPDF is completely ad-free.
03

On-Device Local Storage

How your reading library is maintained

To deliver instant page rendering and preserve your reading progress between sessions, WidgetPDF stores your library preferences directly on your local device.

Private On-Device Storage: All library indexes, reading progress, bookmarks, and widget configurations reside within the app's protected storage space on your device. We do not operate remote servers or cloud storage for your reading materials.
04

Device Permissions & File Access

Transparent explanation of system permissions

WidgetPDF follows the principle of minimal permissions, requesting only access essential for viewing PDF files:

  • Document Selection: When you tap "Import PDF", you interact with Android's secure system file picker. WidgetPDF only receives access to the specific document you choose to open.
  • No Broad Storage Scanning: The app does not request broad file system access and cannot scan your photos, media, or private folders.
  • Home Screen Widgets: Android home screen widgets are updated locally by your device launcher to show your current reading progress.
05

User Control & Data Deletion

Complete authority over your documents

Because WidgetPDF keeps everything on your device, you have direct and instantaneous control:

  • Removing Documents: Deleting a book from your library removes the document, its bookmarks, and reading history immediately.
  • Full Uninstall: If you uninstall WidgetPDF, all local app preferences and widget configurations are automatically purged by your operating system.
06

Children's Privacy

Safe for readers of all ages

WidgetPDF is suitable for readers of all ages. Because the application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, it complies with children's privacy standards including COPPA and international regulations.
