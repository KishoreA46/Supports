Account Management

Account Deletion Guide

Follow the steps below to permanently delete your TradeLift account and all associated data.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone

Once your account is deleted, all your data—including trade history, listings, messages, and profile information—will be permanently removed from our servers. You will not be able to recover your account.

How to Delete Your Account

1

Open TradeLift App

Launch the TradeLift app on your device and make sure you are logged into the account you wish to delete.

2

Navigate to Settings

Tap on your profile icon in the bottom navigation bar, then tap the settings gear icon in the top-right corner.

Profile → Settings
3

Go to Account Settings

Scroll down and tap on "Account" to access your account management options.

Settings → Account
4

Select "Delete Account"

Scroll to the bottom of the Account page and tap the "Delete Account" button, highlighted in red.

Account → Delete Account
5

Confirm Your Identity

For security, you may be asked to re-enter your password or verify your identity via a confirmation code sent to your registered email or phone number.

6

Confirm Deletion

Review the summary of what will be deleted, then tap "Permanently Delete My Account" to finalize the process. Your account and data will be scheduled for deletion.

What Happens After Deletion

🗑️

Data Permanently Removed

Your profile, trade history, listings, saved items, and messages will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

🔐

No Access to Account

You will be immediately logged out and will no longer be able to access your account or any associated data.

⏳

30-Day Grace Period

Some data may be retained for up to 30 days to fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements.

📧

Email Unlinked

Your email address will be unlinked and can be used to create a new account in the future, if desired.

🔄

Fresh Start Available

After deletion, you're welcome to create a brand-new TradeLift account at any time. Previous data will not be recoverable.

🛡️

Anonymized Analytics

Fully anonymized, aggregated usage data that cannot identify you may be retained for analytics and service improvement.
